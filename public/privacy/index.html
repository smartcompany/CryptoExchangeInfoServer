Privacy Policy

Exora (Exora - Crypto Exchange Hub) · Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy describes how the Exora mobile application (“App”) and related backend services (“Service”, hosted at cryptoexchangeinfoserver.vercel.app) handle information.

1. Information we process

  • On your device: cached exchange event/earn data, UI preferences, and local database content. This stays on the device unless you clear app data.
  • Settings fetch: the App may request configuration (ad unit IDs, referral links) from our Service.
  • Optional AI analysis: when you request an event summary, event text (and related metadata) may be sent to our Service and forwarded to Google’s Gemini API. We do not use this to build a permanent personal profile.
  • Ads: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and related SDKs may process advertising identifiers per Google’s policies.

2. Exchange APIs

Event lists, earn products, and images are typically requested directly from your device to third-party exchange public APIs (for example Binance, Bybit, OKX). Those providers process requests under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not operate those exchanges.

3. Accounts

The App does not require creating an Exora account. Referral links may open third-party exchange signup pages.

4. Data retention

Local cache remains until you delete the App or clear data. Server logs for API requests may be retained for a limited operational period. AI prompts are processed to return a response and are not intended as a long-term gallery of your content.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region).
